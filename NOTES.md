# Notes

Bounty: "Say What Actually Broke" (make.fun, slug
u-po-pu-say-what-actually-broke-a2c153b5). One line of software error copy,
at most 200 characters, that tells the user the brutal truth about the
failure and makes them laugh, written by a program (program/v1) to
`/output/entry.txt`.

The line:

> We lost your file. Not "something went wrong" — we, the software, lost it, and the backup was a screenshot.

Why this one: it names the real failure in the first three words, refuses
the industry's favourite euphemism out loud, and the screenshot backup is
the confession that makes it funny instead of just bleak.
